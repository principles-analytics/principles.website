Background

About me

I am the founder of Principles Analytics, an independent advisory practice focused on data strategy and artificial intelligence. I work directly with executives and boards — without the hierarchy of a large consultancy — to help organisations build lasting data capability.

My work sits at the intersection of business strategy and technology. Over the years I have advised companies across industries on everything from building foundational data infrastructure to deploying machine learning at scale and governing AI responsibly.

I am available to engage as a retained strategic advisor, an independent board member, or for focused project-based work. In every engagement, I bring rigorous thinking, an independent perspective, and a genuine commitment to outcomes — not outputs.

  • Analytics organisation design

  • Modern data platform architecture (cloud, lakehouse, mesh)

  • Technology vendor evaluation and procurement

How I work

Guiding principles

The name Principles Analytics is deliberate. Good data work — like good advice — is grounded in principles that outlast any single tool or trend.

Independence

No vendor affiliations, no hidden incentives. My recommendations serve your interests — full stop.

Clarity

Complex data and AI topics translated into clear, actionable insight for leaders at every level.

Pragmatism

Long-term thinking

Building durable capability — not just delivering a report. I measure success by the difference made, not days billed.
